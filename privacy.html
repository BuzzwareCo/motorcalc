Privacy

Privacy policy

MotorCalc is designed to keep your calculations on your device.

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Who is responsible for the app

MotorCalc is provided by Lee Donnellan. For privacy or support questions, email 66cognacs_roan@icloud.com.

Information you enter

You may enter vehicle details, mileage, vehicle-value assumptions and valuation quotes, as well as finance amounts, dates, deposits, interest rates, payment terms and preferences. These details can be personal financial information.

How information is handled

Your calculation, valuation history and app preferences are stored locally in MotorCalc's private storage on your device. MotorCalc does not send this information to the developer, does not provide accounts or a developer cloud-sync service, and does not contain advertising, tracking or third-party analytics.

If you choose to export a backup or share an estimate, iOS sends the selected information to the file location or app that you choose. The developer does not receive the exported file or shared estimate. The recipient or destination you select has its own privacy terms.

Links to other websites

MotorCalc may open independent information websites only when you tap a link. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies. MotorCalc is not affiliated with a lender, valuation provider or independent guidance provider unless it expressly says so.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains until you change it, use Clear all inputs, restore another backup or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its local app container from the device. You are responsible for deleting exported backups or shared copies stored elsewhere.

Apple services

Apple may process App Store transactions, crash diagnostics or device information under Apple's own privacy terms and your device settings. That processing is separate from the finance information stored locally by MotorCalc.

Legal basis and your rights

If you contact the developer, processing your message is necessary to respond to you or is based on legitimate interests in providing support and preventing misuse. Where consent is required, it will be requested separately.

Depending on your circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights to request access to, correction or deletion of personal data, restriction or objection to processing, and data portability. You can exercise these rights by emailing 66cognacs_roan@icloud.com. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The current version will remain available on this page.
