Support

How can I help?

For help, feedback or a problem with MotorCalc, contact the developer directly.

Email support

66cognacs_roan@icloud.com

Do not send account numbers, bank or card details, a complete finance agreement, identity documents or your full address.

Helpful details to include

  • Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.
  • The MotorCalc app version.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • The steps that reproduce the issue.
  • A screenshot with personal and financial details removed where possible.

Important limits

MotorCalc provides planning estimates. For a binding settlement figure, agreement interpretation or a vehicle valuation, contact the relevant lender, dealer or valuation provider.
